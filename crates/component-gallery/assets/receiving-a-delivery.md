# How to Receive a Delivery

When the lorry arrives, open the purchase order and check every crate against
the delivery note before you sign anything. A shortfall is easy to settle at the
gate and much harder a week later.

## Before the Lorry Arrives

1. Open **Purchase Orders** and find the order by its container number.
2. Print the delivery note, or keep it open on a tablet in the yard.
3. Clear enough space in the warehouse for the crates on the note.

> If the container number is missing from the order, ask the office to add it
> before unloading starts. Receiving against the wrong order is the most common
> mistake.

## Counting the Crates

Open the receive screen from the order. Each line shows how many crates were
ordered and how many have been counted so far.

![The receive screen, with the counted crates for each line](/gallery-files/receive-screen.svg "The receive screen")

For each crate:

- Check the crate label matches the SKU on the line, for example `TRVT-024`.
- Look for broken corners or wet packing before you count it as received.
- Tap **Count Crate** once per crate. Don't type the total at the end.

## When the Count Doesn't Match

If you count fewer crates than ordered, write the shortfall on the delivery note
before the driver signs it. Then mark the line as short:

```text
Line B: ordered 13, received 12, 1 crate short
```

The office uses that note to claim the missing crate from the supplier, so
photograph it and attach the photo to the order.

## Finishing Up

Press **Complete Receipt** once every line is counted. The stock appears in the
warehouse straight away, and the order moves to *Received*.
